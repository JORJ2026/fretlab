Use FretLab offline on any device

Install FretLab from this website (it works offline after one visit), or save the single-file Beta below. The desktop apps show FretLab in your browser from a small server that only listens on your own computer, so everything stays offline. They aren't code-signed, so your system may ask you to confirm the first time you open one.
