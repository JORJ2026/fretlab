Analysis workbooks used by the Buckethead Analysis and Matteo Carcassi Analysis pages (ManicRulesGuitar update 57).

Buckethead_bar_by_bar_analysis_v3.xlsx  - bar-by-bar analysis of 7 Buckethead tabs (sheets per tab, patterns, drums, bass, chords, solos, phrase templates).
Carcassi_Opus60_analysis.xlsx           - bar-by-bar analysis of the 25 Etudes, Op. 60 (bars, chords, unique bars, phrase templates, melody, bass, notes).

The MIDI, Guitar Pro and text files are inside the app (beta/bhcc.js) and are offered as downloads on the two pages.
