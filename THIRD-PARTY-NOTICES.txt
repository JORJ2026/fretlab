How the licences fit together
-----------------------------
FretLab as a whole is GPL-3.0-or-later. That works with everything bundled here:
  * DjVu.js is "GPL-2.0 or later", so it may be used under GPL-3.0.
  * SheetJS (Apache-2.0) is compatible with GPL-3.0 (not with GPL-2.0-only, which is why FretLab picks version 3).
  * jsPDF, html2canvas, JSZip (MIT option), docx and Tone.js are MIT, which GPL-3.0 allows.
  * Roboto (SIL OFL 1.1) is a font file shipped alongside, under its own licence.
Because the whole site is served as readable source (no build step, nothing minified apart from the third-party
libraries), the "corresponding source" the GPL asks for is the site itself. Keep the notice lines in each file.
If you later want a non-GPL licence for FretLab, delete original/vendor/djvu.js and its four licence files (see
section 3) and ask every contributor to agree to the change first. This file is a good-faith inventory, not legal advice.
