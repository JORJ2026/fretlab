FretLab privacy

← Back to FretLab

In short

FretLab runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your settings, library, progress, videos or files to any server.

What is stored, and where

Your settings, progress and library (your own scales, chords, progressions and patterns) are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. You can see how much is stored, back it up, or erase it in the Beta under About › Your data. Clearing your browser's site data also erases it.

The offline copy of the app is kept in your browser's cache (and by the installed app) so it works without a connection.

Files you create

Exported videos, MIDI, PDF, tab and backup files are created on your device and saved where you choose. FretLab does not upload them.

Hosting

If you open FretLab from a web address, the company that hosts the site may keep ordinary server logs (such as your IP address and the time of the request), as any web host does. FretLab itself cannot see or control those logs. Downloading the app and running it from a file avoids this.

Children

FretLab collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes it will be stated here and in the app's What's new list.
