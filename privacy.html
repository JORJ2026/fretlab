ManicRulesGuitar privacy

← Back to ManicRulesGuitar

In short

ManicRulesGuitar runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your settings, library, progress, videos or files to any server.

What is stored, and where

Your settings, progress and library (your own scales, chords, progressions and patterns) are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. You can see how much is stored, back it up, or erase it in the Beta under About › Your data. Clearing your browser's site data also erases it.

The offline copy of the app is kept in your browser's cache (and by the installed app) so it works without a connection.

Files you create

Exported videos, MIDI, PDF, tab and backup files are created on your device and saved where you choose. ManicRulesGuitar does not upload them.

Hosting

If you open ManicRulesGuitar from a web address, the company that hosts the site may keep ordinary server logs (such as your IP address and the time of the request), as any web host does. ManicRulesGuitar itself cannot see or control those logs. Downloading the app and running it from a file avoids this.

Children

ManicRulesGuitar collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Known limitations

  • Everything is stored only in this browser on this device. Clearing the browser's site data, or using a private window, deletes it, so use About › Your data › Back up now regularly.
  • Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac may delete saved data from websites you have not opened for about a week. Installing the app to the Home Screen, or keeping a backup file, avoids losing it.
  • Sound starts only after your first tap or key press (a browser rule).
  • The tuner needs microphone permission and a secure (https) address; it is only asked for when you start the tuner.
  • MIDI keyboard input works in Chrome and Edge, not in Safari or Firefox.
  • Video export depends on the browser's recording support and is slow on phones; long exports can take minutes.
  • The How-to guide PDF was written under the app's old name, FretLab.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere, so there is no sync between devices: move data with a backup file.

Changes

If this ever changes it will be stated here and in the app's What's new list.
